# Omega Journal — Support

*Page source for https://unknownprojects.net/omega-journal/support.*

Need help? Email **support@unknownprojects.net** *(TO CONFIRM before publishing)*. Tell us your
macOS version and the Omega Journal version (menu **Omega Journal → About Omega Journal**).
We usually reply within two business days.

Omega Journal has no accounts and no servers, so we can't see your journal. That also means we
can't recover it for you, so please keep backups (see below).

## Getting started

- **Write**: click **New Entry** (⌘N). Entries save automatically as you type.
- **Organize**: add tags and a mood, file entries into notebooks, or build smart folders from a search.
- **Find**: ⌘F searches everything. Try operators like `tag:work`, `mood:good`, `before:2026-01-01`, `has:image`.
- **Command palette**: ⌘K jumps anywhere.

## Privacy features

- **Hidden entries**: hide an entry from its **More** menu. Hidden entries show as masked cards until you unlock with Touch ID or your Mac password. ⌘L locks them again.
- **Encryption**: entry text, attachments and backups are encrypted on your Mac. Titles, tags, moods and dates stay readable on disk so search stays fast. Protect your Mac account with a password and FileVault.
- Read the full [privacy policy](https://unknownprojects.net/omega-journal/privacy).

## Voice memos

The first time you record, macOS asks for microphone access. If you said no, open **System
Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone** and turn on **Omega Journal**, then try again.

## Backups and moving to a new Mac

- The app keeps **seven daily backups** automatically.
- **Settings → Data & Backups** can make a backup now, add an extra backup folder (for example on an external drive), verify backups and restore.
- To move to a new Mac, use **Settings → Data & Backups → Password-Protected Export** (an `.ojenc` file), then import that file on the new Mac. Remember the passphrase: it can't be recovered. Automatic backups are tied to this Mac's Keychain key and can't be opened elsewhere on their own.

## Used Omega Journal before the App Store version?

If you used an earlier version of Omega Journal on this Mac, the App Store version brings your
journal across automatically the first time it opens. If your entries don't appear:

1. Quit Omega Journal.
2. Open **Settings → Data & Backups → Import existing journal…** and choose your previous
   `OmegaJournal` folder (it's in `~/Library/Application Support/` in Finder; press ⌘⇧G and paste that path).

## Common questions

**The app asks for my login password about "confidential information".** That's macOS asking whether
Omega Journal may read its own encryption key from your Keychain. Choose **Always Allow**.

**Can I sync between Macs or with iPhone?** Not yet. Your journal stays on one Mac.

**How do I delete everything?** Delete the app, then in Finder go to `~/Library/Containers/` and move the
"Omega Journal" folder to the Trash.

**Refunds** are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
